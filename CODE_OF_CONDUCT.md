# Code of Conduct

This is a small public documentation repository. The expectation is simple:
be precise, be honest about shipped vs proposed, and do not use this space
to harm people or systems.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Do not pressure maintainers to publish private source, secrets, or
  internal attack-surface maps.
- Do not discuss unauthorized targeting of third-party networks.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, hate, or threats.
- Publishing secrets, customer data, or exploit material.
- Social-engineering maintainers for access to `app.akinsec.com` tenants.

## Enforcement

The repository owner (GitHub: [dfalt0](https://github.com/dfalt0)) may edit
or remove content and block accounts. Reports: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This document is a practical policy for a one-maintainer docs repo, not a
full Contributor Covenant legal instrument.
